---
title: Splitting an apple by hand is about leverage
tags: [physics, everyday-science, technique]
---

Splitting an apple with bare hands requires strength, but grip and leverage determine how effectively that strength is used.

**The technique**

1. Dry your hands and the apple, and remove the stem.
2. Hold the apple with the stem indentation facing up.
3. Place both thumbs beside each other in the indentation, with your fingers wrapped around opposite sides.
4. Press your thumbs into the hollow while rolling your hands outward—like opening a book.
5. Once a crack starts, continue pulling the halves apart.

Why it works

The aim is to initiate a crack and pull it open. Squeezing the entire apple spreads force broadly; the opening motion concentrates stress where you want the split to begin.

Apple firmness, shape, and grip all affect difficulty. A failed attempt does not necessarily mean insufficient strength.

The broader lesson: before applying more force, check where and how you are applying it.
